Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about domains and registration.

What is a TLD?

TLD stands for Top-Level Domain. It's the last part of a domain name, like .com, .net, or .org.

How do I transfer my domain?

Transferring is easy! Just unlock your domain at your current registrar and provide us with the authorization code.

Can I hide my personal information?

Yes, with our Domain Privacy Protection, your personal details are replaced with generic information in the WHOIS database.
